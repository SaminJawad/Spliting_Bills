How to use BillSplit

  1. Enter bill detailsAdd an optional label, the total bill amount, tax percentage, and a tip amount.
  2. Add membersType each person's name and tap the + button to build the group.
  3. Choose a split methodUse Equal Split to divide the grand total evenly, or Item-based to assign item prices to specific people.
  4. Select who paidChoose whether everyone paid their own share, one person paid everything, or multiple people contributed upfront.
  5. Calculate and reviewTap Calculate Split to see each person's share and any settlement amounts, then save it to history if needed.
